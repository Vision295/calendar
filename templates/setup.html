Welcome to Your Personal Scheduler!

This website helps you plan and visualize your weekly tasks alongside your existing calendar.

Start by filling out the setup form with your tasks or activities. For each task, you can specify:

  • Name – what the task is called
  • Hours per week – how many hours you want to dedicate
  • Priority level – lower numbers mean higher importance
  • Deadline – when the task needs to be completed
  • Maximum consecutive hours per day – how long you can work on it in a single session

Once you save your setup, the website will automatically schedule your tasks within your available time slots, respecting working hours and daily limits.
